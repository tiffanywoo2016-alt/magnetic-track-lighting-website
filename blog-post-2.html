Mastering Smart Lighting Control: DALI, 0-10V, and Wireless Protocols

By Tiffany Woo • May 30, 2026 • 5 min read

Choosing the right control protocol is one of the most important decisions in a magnetic track lighting project. The protocol determines how deeply your fixtures dim, whether you can address each module individually, and how easily the system integrates with building management or smart home platforms.

Here is a practical comparison of the three most common protocols for magnetic track lighting — DALI dimming, 0-10V LED track control, and Casambi wireless — so you can match the right system to your project type.

Quick Answer

DALI dimming — best for large commercial projects where each fixture needs individual addressability. Supports flicker-free dimming down to 0.1% and two-way communication for status reporting. Ideal for hotels, galleries and smart offices.

0-10V LED track control — a simple analog dimming method. Low cost, widely compatible with existing building management systems. Suitable for basic offices and retail where group dimming is sufficient.

Casambi wireless track control — professional-grade Bluetooth mesh control. No gateway required for small installations. Preferred by lighting designers for luxury retail, high-end villas and art galleries where aesthetic control matters.

DALI Dimming for Magnetic Track Lighting: Precision and Addressability

DALI (Digital Addressable Lighting Interface) is a digital protocol where each light module has its own bus address. This enables individual control of every fixture on the track — you can dim one spotlight to 30%, another to 70%, and a third off, all from the same control bus.

For large-scale magnetic track installations in hotels or mixed-use buildings, DALI dimming is the industry standard. The two-way communication also means the system can report lamp failures and power consumption back to a central building management system.

0-10V LED Track Control: Simpler, Lower Cost

0-10V is an analog dimming method where a control voltage between 0 and 10 volts determines the light output. It is simpler and cheaper than DALI, but all fixtures on the same control line dim together — there is no individual addressability.

For projects where group dimming is acceptable — a row of flood lights in a corridor, or a set of spotlights over a retail display — 0-10V LED track control is a reliable, cost-effective choice.

Casambi Wireless Track Control: No Gateway, Full Flexibility

Casambi uses Bluetooth Low Energy mesh to create a wireless lighting control network. Each module has an embedded Casambi chip, eliminating the need for a central gateway in small to medium installations.

For renovation projects where running control wiring is impractical, Casambi wireless track control is often the best option. It supports smooth dimming down to 1% and can be controlled via a smartphone app or dedicated wall switches.

FAQ: Smart Lighting Control for Magnetic Track Systems

Can I use DALI and Casambi together in the same project?
Yes, with a DALI-to-Casambi gateway. This hybrid approach is used in projects where wired DALI runs the main zones for stability, while Casambi controls accent or decorative zones that need flexible scene changes via mobile app.
Does 0-10V dimming work with all magnetic track LED modules?
Not all modules support 0-10V dimming. The module must have a driver with a 0-10V interface. Check the module specification before specifying. Most premium modules with built-in drivers support 0-10V as a standard option.
What is the maximum distance for Casambi Bluetooth control in a track lighting installation?
Casambi devices form a mesh network, so each module extends the range. In a typical commercial installation with modules spaced 1-3 metres apart, the effective range covers the entire floor. For very large spaces, add a Casambi router to bridge zones.
