Available in three installation configurations for different ceiling types. All tracks operate on a 48V DC system with robust magnetic holding force suitable for commercial environments.

Installation Methods

Choose the right mounting for your project

Each 35mm track model is designed for specific ceiling conditions and project requirements. Select based on construction phase, ceiling type and desired finish.

Surface Mounted

WM990

Track is mounted directly onto the ceiling surface. Ideal for renovation projects where ceiling modification is not possible or desired.

  • No ceiling cutting required
  • Quick installation, minimal disruption
  • Fully accessible for maintenance
  • Best for concrete / solid ceilings

Recessed with Trim

WK990

Track is recessed into the ceiling with a visible trim border. Provides a neat finished edge and is easier to install than trimless options.

  • Clean finished edge with trim
  • Suitable for suspended ceilings
  • Easier installation and alignment
  • Best for commercial fit-outs

Trimless Recessed

WY990

Track is recessed flush with the ceiling for a seamless, invisible integration. Requires installation during ceiling construction phase.

  • Flush, seamless ceiling finish
  • Premium architectural appearance
  • Installed during construction
  • Best for high-end commercial projects
Connectors & Accessories

Complete your track layout

A full range of connectors, power inputs and end caps for building L-shape, T-shape, cross and straight-line configurations with the 35mm system.

└ L-Shape Connector

90° corner connector for changing track direction. Maintains continuous electrical and magnetic contact through the turn.

├ T-Shape Connector

Three-way junction connector for branching the track layout. Enables flexible grid and perimeter configurations.

┼ Cross Connector

Four-way intersection connector for complex grid layouts in large commercial ceiling areas.

⏹ End Cap

Terminates the track run with a clean finish. Provides electrical isolation at the end of each track section.

⚡ Power Input Module

Connects the 48V DC driver to the track. Available as straight input or corner input module for flexible power feed positioning.

🔗 Suspension Kit

Cable suspension hardware for pendant mounting configurations. Supports track weight with adjustable drop length.
