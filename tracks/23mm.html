23mm Ultra-Slim Track System

Ultra-slim private-module magnetic track system with 4 installation methods — surface, suspended, recessed ceiling and open-cut. Compatible with 23Y series LED modules.

System Overview

Four installation methods, one premium platform

The 23mm system is engineered for modern residential and premium interior projects, offering the slimmest profile in the Tracklinear lineup with four versatile installation options.

▬ Ultra-Slim Surface

Ultra-compact 24×7mm profile, ideal for modern minimalist interiors where space is limited. Discrete surface mounting with a near-invisible footprint.

  • Profile: 24 × 7 mm
  • Lengths: 1M / 2M / 3M
  • Color: Black / White
  • Best for: Minimalist residential, low-profile ceilings

⬜ Surface/Suspended

Robust 27×37mm profile for both direct ceiling mount and pendant suspension. Versatile installation for rooms with varying ceiling conditions.

  • Profile: 27 × 37 mm
  • Lengths: 1M / 2M / 3M
  • Color: Black / White
  • Best for: Living rooms, bedrooms, suspended layouts

⊟ Recessed Ceiling

60×37mm recessed profile for clean ceiling integration in residential and light commercial applications. Flush-mounted for a seamless finish.

  • Profile: 60 × 37 mm
  • Lengths: 1M / 2M / 3M
  • Color: Black / White
  • Best for: New construction, plasterboard ceilings

⊞ Open-Cut

40×37mm open-cut profile for plasterboard ceiling integration, providing a seamless finished look. Designed for installations where trimless appearance is desired.

  • Profile: 40 × 37 mm
  • Lengths: 1M / 2M / 3M
  • Color: Black / White
  • Best for: Premium residential, seamless ceilings

Connectors & Accessories

Complete your track layout

Full range of connectors, power input modules and joiners for building straight runs, corners and complex layouts with the 23mm system — or request a complete 48V magnetic track system quotation matched by our engineers.

↳ L-Shape Connector

90° corner adapter for 23mm tracks. Maintains continuous electrical and magnetic contact through directional changes in your layout.

⎓ 7-Shape Connector

180° Z-shape transition connector for offset track runs. Enables parallel track configurations with precise spacing.

→ Straight Input Module

Power input with straight feed. Connects the 48V DC driver to the track at the start or end of a run for clean power integration.

↴ Corner Power Module

Power input with corner connection. Combines power feed and 90° corner turn in a single accessory for compact layouts.

— Direct Connector

In-line track joiner for extending straight runs. Provides rigid mechanical connection and continuous electrical conductivity.

⚡ Clip Connector

Quick-connect clip joiner for fast installation and tool-free track assembly. Ideal for rapid deployment and modular layouts.
