MAGNETRACK / Tracks / 20mm Track

20mm Magnetic Track System

Surface mounted, trimless recessed and board recessed 20mm track rails for residential and commercial magnetic track lighting projects. Compatible with 22Y and 20B series LED modules.

Track Options

Three Installation Methods

Choose the right 20mm track for your ceiling type and project requirements.

▭ Surface Mounted

Direct ceiling mount, no cutting needed. Ideal for renovation projects and concrete ceilings. Quick installation with minimal preparation work.

  • Thickness: 1.5mm / 2.0mm
  • Colors: Black / White
  • Best for: Renovations, concrete ceilings, cost-sensitive projects

⊟ Trimless Recessed

Seamless ceiling integration for premium interiors. Perfect for projects where a clean, uninterrupted ceiling surface is required.

  • Thickness: 1.6mm
  • Colors: Black
  • Best for: Villas, hotels, high-end residences

⊞ Board Recessed

Neat edge for board ceilings in commercial and residential projects. Provides a clean finish that integrates with drywall or panel ceilings.

  • Thickness: 1.6mm / 2.5mm
  • Colors: Black
  • Best for: Commercial spaces, offices, residential board ceilings

Accessories

Connectors & Accessories

Complete your 20mm track system with matching connectors, power modules and mounting accessories.

∟ L-Shape Connector

90° corner connection for continuous track runs around room perimeters or creating L-shaped layouts.

✚ Cross Connector

4-way junction for complex ceiling layouts. Enables grid-style track configurations for multi-directional lighting.

┬ T-Shape Connector

3-way junction for branching track runs. Ideal for creating T-intersections in open-plan ceiling designs.

⌞ Wall Corner Connector

Ceiling-to-wall transition connector. Enables track to turn from the ceiling onto a wall for vertical lighting applications.

⚡ Power Input Module

Straight input module for connecting the 48V DC driver to the track system. Available in end-feed and middle-feed configurations.

⏤ End Caps & Suspension Kits

Finish and mount track ends. End caps provide a clean termination while suspension kits enable pendant mounting for high ceilings.
