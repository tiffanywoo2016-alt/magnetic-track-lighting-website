Applications

Where to Use 20mm Track Lighting

Flexible layouts that adapt to living rooms, kitchens, hallways and home offices — with commercial options for retail and hospitality.

🛋 Living Room

Linear flood modules for even ambient light, plus adjustable spots to highlight art, shelving or a TV wall. Easy to reposition when you rearrange the furniture.

🍳 Kitchen

Task lighting above counters and islands with no visible cables. Trimless or recessed tracks blend into the ceiling for a clean, modern finish.

🚪 Hallway & Entryway

Narrow tracks run the full length of a corridor without clutter. Low-profile surface tracks suit renovation projects with no ceiling work.

💡 Home Office

Adjustable spots reduce glare on screens and bring focused light to work surfaces. Choose CRI ≥90 modules for comfortable, accurate light.

🏬 Retail & Hospitality

For shops, cafes and hotels, combine flood, grille and spot modules on one track to switch between ambient and accent lighting as the space changes.

🖼 Gallery & Display

Precise beam angles (24° spots) put focused light exactly on artwork, products or shelves — perfect for showrooms and display walls.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About
20mm Magnetic Track Systems

Practical answers for importers, contractors and lighting specifiers working on 48V magnetic track lighting projects.

01
How many LED fixtures can a single 20mm magnetic track support without voltage drop? +

A single 20mm 48V magnetic track can usually support around 120–150W per power feed, depending on track length, conductor design, fixture wattage, and feed position. For example, one feed may support about 8–12 pcs of 10W magnetic light modules or 6–8 pcs of 15–20W modules.

For longer runs or higher total wattage, we recommend mid-point feeding, dual-end feeding, or separate power zones to reduce voltage drop and keep brightness stable.

02
Are 20mm magnetic tracks from different brands interchangeable with the same LED modules? +

Not always. Although many systems are called 20mm magnetic track, different brands may use different conductor positions, magnetic contacts, locking structures, and fixture connection designs.

Our 20mm magnetic track is designed to match most standard 20mm magnetic lighting modules on the market. However, for project orders or bulk purchasing, we always recommend sample testing before mass production to confirm compatibility.

03
Does a 20mm magnetic track support DALI or 0-10V dimming? +

The 20mm magnetic track itself is mainly a 48V low-voltage power rail. Whether the system supports DALI, 0-10V, TRIAC, smart control, or simple on/off switching depends on the selected driver and control system.

For commercial projects such as hotels, offices, retail stores, and showrooms, we can match compatible DALI dimmable drivers, 0-10V drivers, or smart control solutions according to the project requirements.
