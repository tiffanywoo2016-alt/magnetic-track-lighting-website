Where Should You Place the Driver for 48V Magnetic Track Lighting?

By Tiffany Woo • June 25, 2026 • 6 min read

In many 48V magnetic track projects, the track and light modules are selected correctly, but the installation still becomes difficult because the 48V magnetic track driver placement and power feed were not planned before ceiling work started.

The result: last-minute ceiling cuts, difficult maintenance access, voltage drop, or expensive rework.

This guide covers where to place the driver, how to plan the power feed position, and what to confirm before installation — so your project runs smoothly from the first fix.

Why Driver Position Matters Before Installation

A 48V LED driver is not something you can hide anywhere. Its position affects several critical aspects of the installation:

  • Space requirement. A typical 150–300W driver is not a small component — it measures roughly 280–350mm long, 60–80mm wide, and 35–45mm high — so it needs a dedicated enclosure or ceiling cavity with enough space.
  • Heat dissipation. Drivers generate heat during operation. Enclosed in a tight space without airflow, they overheat, reduce lifespan, or trigger thermal protection.
  • Maintenance access. Drivers can fail. If the driver is buried inside a sealed ceiling, replacing it means cutting through drywall. An access panel solves this at the framing stage for almost nothing — adding one later costs 10× more.
  • Cable length to the track. The distance between driver and track feed point adds resistance. A long cable run means voltage drop before the power even reaches the track — reducing overall track lighting driver position efficiency.
  • Dimming and control wiring. DALI, 0-10V, and smart controls require extra control wires between the driver and the control system. These must be routed during installation.
  • Ceiling type. Recessed and trimless ceilings are the most difficult to modify after installation. Once closed, adding a driver access point or relocating a feed requires cutting into finished surfaces.

Common Driver Positions for 48V Magnetic Track Lighting

Driver Position Suitable For Notes
Inside ceiling with access panel Hotels, villas, retail projects Best for maintenance — easy to service when needed
Inside track cavity Clean appearance, small systems Limited space — typically max 60–100W
Near track end Short simple runs Easy wiring, but longer distance increases LED track driver location voltage drop risk
Central electrical box Large commercial projects Needs proper 48V track circuit planning and distribution
Cabinet / service area Retail and hospitality Easy access — works if distance to track is under 2–3m
Never permanently seal the driver in a ceiling area without maintenance access. This is especially important for trimless ceilings, recessed track, hotel corridors, villa living rooms, and retail ceilings — where cutting into the finished surface later is disruptive and expensive.

How to Choose the Power Feed Position

The power feed position determines how far voltage travels along the track and how evenly fixtures perform. It should be decided together with the driver location, because the feed point affects cable routing, track length, fixture concentration, and ceiling structure.

  • End feed — Power enters at one end. Simple, suitable for short runs.
  • Mid-point feed — Power enters at the centre. Voltage travels half the distance in each direction.
  • Dual-end feed — Power enters from both ends. Best for long or high-load runs.

The right magnetic track power feed position depends on where the driver is placed, how the cable runs are routed, where fixtures are concentrated on the track, and what ceiling structure allows.

One Track Run Does Not Always Mean One Circuit

This is one of the most commonly overlooked details in commercial track lighting circuit projects.

A long magnetic track may look continuous on the ceiling, but electrically it can be divided into several circuits — each with its own driver, its own feed, and its own control group.

Project Situation Suggested Circuit Planning
3–4m short track 1 driver / 1 circuit
6–8m medium track 1–2 circuits depending on total wattage
10–12m long track 2–3 circuits recommended
Retail store or showroom Divide by lighting zones, not track length
Hotel corridor Split by area for easier maintenance

Splitting a long track into multiple circuits gives you higher total load capacity, independent zone control, redundancy if one driver fails, and smaller drivers that are easier to install and maintain.

Dimming and Smart Control Must Be Confirmed Before Wiring

The dimming method must be confirmed before the driver is selected and wiring is routed. Each method requires different driver compatibility and control wiring:

  • Non-dim / On-Off
  • DALI (needs 2 extra control wires)
  • 0-10V (analogue — needs 2 control wires)
  • Triac (phase-cut — common in retrofit)
  • Tuya / Zigbee (wireless — no extra control wires, needs gateway placement)
  • CCT tunable (needs separate controller per channel)
  • Separate group control (each zone needs its own control pair)

Planning dimmable magnetic track wiring at the start is far cheaper than pulling extra cables after the ceiling is closed.

Common Mistakes That Cause Rework

  1. Driver hidden without access panel. A failed driver in a sealed ceiling costs hours of labour to replace. An access panel costs 10 minutes to install at framing stage.
  2. Driver too far from the track. Long cable runs add voltage drop before power even reaches the track. Keep the driver accessibility track lighting distance under 2–3m.
  3. Long track powered from one end only. 5m+ runs on end feed will have noticeable dimness at the far end. Use mid-point or dual-end feed.
  4. Dimming method confirmed after wiring. Choosing DALI after standard wiring is installed means pulling new cables through finished ceilings.
  5. No spare capacity for future lights. A fully loaded driver leaves no room for expansion. Leave 20% headroom.

FAQ: 48V Track Driver and Power Feed Planning

Can the driver be hidden inside the ceiling without an access panel?
Not recommended. Drivers generate heat and may fail. Always plan an access panel for maintenance, especially in recessed or trimless ceiling installations.
How far can the driver be from the magnetic track?
Keep the distance under 2–3 metres. Longer cable runs add resistance and voltage drop, which reduces fixture brightness and may cause uneven lighting at the far end.
Can one driver power two separate track sections?
Yes, if the combined load is within the driver's rated capacity and both sections are wired into the same circuit. Confirm with a magnetic track pre-installation checklist review before fixing cable routes.
