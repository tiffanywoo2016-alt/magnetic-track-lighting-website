Does Every Magnetic Track Light Need a Driver?

594| 595|

Not always — and that's the point.

596| 597|

The choice between built-in driver and no-driver modules comes down to your customer's market position and budget, not the project type.

598| 599|

If your customer is targeting the premium segment — hotels, showrooms, high-end residential — built-in driver modules are the right choice. They deliver flicker-free dimming, consistent brightness, and longer lifespan.

600| 601|

If your customer is cost-sensitive — warehouses, utility areas, budget projects — no-driver modules offer a lower upfront cost while still working on the same 48V track system.

602| 603|

Both module types snap onto the same track. The physical installation is identical. The difference is in internal electronics, and that difference determines your customer's long-term satisfaction.

604| 605|

The advantage of working with us: you can offer both. One supplier, two price tiers, one track system.
