# Blog Opener Pattern Rotation Guide

**Purpose:** Prevent content fatigue when writing consecutive B2B blog posts. Each opener pattern should be used at most 2 times per 4 posts.

## 6 Opener Patterns

### 1. Customer Story
Opens with a real visitor/factory interaction.
- Blog 7: Russian importer asked at production line
- Blog 8: Turkish wholesaler asked about pricing
- Best for: E-E-A-T Experience injection, trust building
- Max consecutive use: 2 posts, then rotate

### 2. Data-Driven ⭐ (est. 2026-06-19)
Opens with a hard fact, statistic, or quantified statement.
- Pattern: "选错安装方式，项目成本增加 30%，工期延长 2 周。这不是估算——是我们在 200+ 个项目中看到的数据。"
- Works well in English too: "Wrong installation method adds 30% cost and 2 weeks delay. This is not an estimate — it is what we see across 200+ magnetic track lighting projects."
- Best for: B2B buyers, project decision-makers
- Google AIO: Facts are highly quotable — AI Overview extracts quantified statements more readily than qualitative ones
- **GEO advantage:** Data-driven openers work for non-comparison articles too (e.g., Blog 9 was a 3-way guide, not a binary comparison). The stat establishes authority without a customer story.
- Pairs well with: comparison tables, spec data

### 3. Trend/Tension
Opens with an industry shift or emerging question.
- Blog 3: "Why trimless is the new standard"
- Best for: thought leadership, design-forward topics

### 4. Direct Question
Opens with the FAQ the article will answer.
- Blog 1: "48V vs 24V — which is right?"
- Best for: comparison/choice articles, high-search-intent topics

### 5. Scenario Classification
Opens by framing a set of distinct scenarios.
- Blog 5: "5 commercial project types, each needing a different approach"
- Best for: educational guides, selection frameworks

### 6. Case Study
Opens with a named project/venue and its before/after.
- Blog 6: Lisbon Boutique Hotel
- Best for: proof-of-concept, credibility building

## Rotation Rule

Consecutive blog posts should NOT share the same opener pattern more than 2 times. After 2 consecutive posts with Pattern 1 (Customer Story), the 3rd post MUST switch to a different pattern.

## Detection

If user says "会不会审美疲劳" or "每篇文章都这么开头" — the opener pattern has been repeated too much. Switch immediately to a different pattern for the next post.
