# Blog Content Quality Assessment: What Makes an Article Google-Favored

> Captured from user's evaluation query on Blog 6 (2026-06-17). User asked "Blog 6 这种文章应该是 Google 喜欢收录和推荐的，你觉得呢？" — indicating she wants to identify which of her articles have high Google potential and understand why.

## Core Principle

Not all B2B blog content is equal in Google's eyes. Google ranks content based on **E-E-A-T signals** (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness), **content density** (specificity of claims), and **structured data compliance**.

## The 5-Factor Google-Favored Assessment Framework

When the user asks "does Google like this article?" or "which article is best for SEO?", evaluate against these 5 factors:

### 1. Case Study / Real Project Data (Highest Signal)
Google trusts content that includes:
- ✅ Real location names ("42-room boutique hotel, Lisbon")
- ✅ Real client personas ("Marco Silva, Owner, Casa do Largo")
- ✅ Specific timelines ("3 renovation phases", "Q1")
- ✅ Unambiguous metrics ("34% energy reduction", "4.7/5.0 ambiance score")
- **→ This is the highest E-E-A-T signal a B2B blog can produce**

### 2. Content Density (Data Richness)
How many **specific, checkable claims** per paragraph?
- ✅ Spec tables (CRI 95+, DALI, UGR<19, 2700K/3000K)
- ✅ Space-by-space breakdown tables (5 zones × 4 parameters)
- ✅ Before/After numerical comparisons
- **Google ranks dense content higher than verbose content** — one spec table beats three paragraphs of prose

### 3. Structured Data Completeness
- ✅ Article Schema with headline, description, image, dates, author, publisher
- ✅ BreadcrumbList Schema
- ✅ BlogPosting Schema on index card
- ✅ OG/Twitter Card tags

### 4. Featured Snippet Structure
Does the article have natural snippet-bait sections?
- ✅ The Problem → Solution → Results (Google LOVES this format)
- ✅ Project/Before/After/Result summary cards (4-card grid is Google-extractable)
- ✅ FAQ-style opening paragraph
- ✅ Clear H2 headers that match search query intent

### 5. Long-Tail Keyword Coverage (Depth)
How many distinct B2B buyer search intents does the article cover?
- Example from Blog 6: covers *hotel lighting*, *magnetic track hotel spec*, *trimless recessed hospitality*, *DALI hospitality control*, *energy savings hotel lighting*
- Target: 5-8 distinct long-tail phrases per article

## User Preference Signal

When the user asks "which is Google-favored?", she wants:
1. A clear YES/NO assessment (not vague hedging like "it should do well")
2. Concrete reasons why (she's learning to identify Google signals herself)
3. Suggestions for improvement (if any — but don't force changes if content is already strong)

**Apply this framework when the user asks about specific article quality or comparative article SEO potential.**
