Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Get professional answers about magnetic track lighting system planning and technical details.

What components are needed for a complete system?

A complete system consists of four key parts: Magnetic Track Rails (Recessed, Surface, or Pendant), LED Light Modules (Spotlights, Grille lights, or Floodlights), DC 48V Power Driver, and Accessories (Connectors, End caps, and Joiners).

Which installation method should I choose?

Choose Trimless Recessed for a seamless look in premium villas/hotels (installed during ceiling construction). Choose Surface Mounted if you don't want to cut the ceiling, or Pendant for high offices and showrooms.

Does the system support smart control?

Yes. We support professional DALI, 0-10V, and Triac dimming. For wireless smart homes, we offer integration with Tuya Zigbee, Casambi, and Bluetooth Mesh.
