Knowledge Base

5 questions B2B buyers ask before ordering

Real technical and commercial answers about magnetic track lighting system selection, voltage drop, sampling, customisation and lead times.

01 How do I select the right track width, module type and driver combination for my project? +
Track width (20/23/35mm) depends on your module requirements and ceiling layout. We check total wattage, track length and dimming preference before recommending a driver. A project drawing or layout sketch helps us match the system precisely to avoid on-site compatibility issues.
02 How many metres of track can one 48V driver support before voltage drop affects performance? +
A single 48V driver typically supports 10–15 metres of track depending on total wattage load. For longer runs or higher power, we recommend multiple power feeds or additional drivers. We calculate voltage drop per layout during quotation to ensure consistent brightness across all modules.
03 Can I test samples before placing a bulk order, and how does the process work for international buyers? +
Yes. We offer sample orders for standard modules and track sections. Sample cost is deductible from your first bulk order. We ship via DHL or FedEx (3–5 working days) with full tracking. If adjustments are needed after sample review, we modify before production.
04 What customization options do you offer for distributors — track length, colour finish, logo and packaging? +
Custom track lengths cut to specification, RAL-matched colour finishes, laser-engraved or printed logo on modules and track, plus private-label packaging with your brand. MOQ varies by customisation type. We also support neutral products for distributors entering new markets.
05 What are realistic lead times for standard and custom orders shipped internationally? +
Stock items ship within 3–7 working days. Custom or OEM production takes 20–30 days depending on quantity. Sea freight to Europe takes 25–35 days, air freight 5–8 days. We provide shipping options and estimated delivery dates before order confirmation.
