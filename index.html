Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Get professional answers about magnetic track lighting system planning and technical details.

What certifications do your products have?

Our magnetic track lighting products are certified with CE and RoHS, compliant with European safety and environmental standards. For other regional certifications, please contact us with your project requirements.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ varies by product line. For standard track rails and modules, we can accommodate smaller quantities for sample and trial orders. For OEM/private-label production, minimum quantities apply — contact us with your target quantity for a quotation.

Can I get samples before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We offer sample orders for most standard products. Sample cost is charged upfront and refundable on bulk orders. Shipping is at the buyer's expense. This allows you to evaluate the quality and finish before committing to volume.

What is the typical lead time?

For standard in-stock items, we ship within 3-7 working days. For OEM/ODM production, lead time is typically 20-30 days depending on order complexity and quantity. We'll confirm exact timing at quotation stage.

Do you offer custom track lengths and color finishes?

Yes. We provide custom track lengths cut to your project specifications, and custom color finishes (white, black, silver, champagne, or RAL-matched). Minimum order quantities apply for custom finishes. Contact us with your requirements.

What's the difference between 20mm, 23mm, and 35mm tracks?

These refer to the track width. 20mm is compact and ideal for residential and light commercial. 23mm is the most common size, compatible with a wide range of modules. 35mm is heavy-duty for high-wattage modules and large commercial spaces. All three run on the same 48V system but use different module form factors.

Is 48V magnetic track lighting safe for commercial use?

Yes. 48V DC is classified as Extra Low Voltage (ELV) under international safety standards. It poses no electric shock risk, requires no licensed electrician for module changes, and is fully compliant for hotels, retail, offices, and public spaces.

Do you offer OEM / ODM services for overseas buyers?

Yes. We provide full OEM and ODM services: custom branding, packaging design, specification sheets with your logo, and private-label production. Minimum order quantities depend on the product line. Contact us with your requirements for a tailored quotation.
