About Us

Your Partner in Magnetic Track Lighting

Tracklinear is a professional manufacturer of 48V magnetic track lighting systems — from track rails and LED modules to smart drivers and accessories. We serve contractors, distributors, interior designers, and lighting specifiers worldwide with reliable OEM/ODM solutions.

Founded 2017 Foshan, China 30+ Export Markets CE & RoHS Certified OEM / ODM Specialist
Our Mission

Making Magnetic Track Lighting Procurement Simpler for B2B Buyers

Magnetic track lighting is a modular, flexible system — but that flexibility means nothing if the track, module, driver, and controller don't work as one. Too often, buyers receive components that don't match, leading to installation delays and costly reorders.

Tracklinear was built to solve this. We match every component as a complete 48V system before shipping, so our customers receive a solution that works out of the box — whether it's a small boutique hotel or a multi-floor commercial project.

Our team brings years of hands-on experience in LED manufacturing, electrical engineering, and cross-border B2B trade. We understand the quality standards and documentation requirements expected by professional buyers in Europe, the Americas, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.

Our Background

Built by Industry Professionals, for Professional Buyers

Tracklinear was founded in 2017 by a team with deep roots in the LED lighting industry. Our founders previously worked in LED chip manufacturing, driver electronics, and international trade — giving us a complete view of the supply chain from component level to finished product.

Located in Foshan, Guangdong — the heart of China's lighting manufacturing hub — we are surrounded by a mature ecosystem of extrusion plants, PCB fabricators, and testing laboratories. This location allows us to maintain tight quality control while keeping production costs efficient for our B2B partners.

Our team includes electrical engineers, quality control specialists, and export sales professionals. We speak English, Mandarin, and Cantonese, and we are experienced in working with buyers from different cultural and business backgrounds.
