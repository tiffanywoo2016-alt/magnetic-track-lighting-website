About Us

A Manufacturer Built for B2B Buyers

48V magnetic track lighting is modular by design. But a system only works when the track, modules, driver, power feeds, connectors and control method are specified together.

Tracklinear supplies complete 48V magnetic track systems for lighting distributors, project contractors and OEM lighting brands.

Before quotation, we review the track profile, installation method, module compatibility, total load, driver capacity, power-feed position and control requirements. We then prepare the order against a clear BOM, rather than treating each component as a separate item.

This helps buyers reduce incomplete quotations, component mismatch and unnecessary reorders.

Our range includes surface-mounted, recessed, trimless and pendant options for retail, hospitality, office, showroom and residential projects. We also support sample testing, private-label packaging, selected customisation and project documentation where required.

If you are preparing a project or building a magnetic track product range, send us your layout or existing BOM. We will help identify what needs to be confirmed before quotation.
