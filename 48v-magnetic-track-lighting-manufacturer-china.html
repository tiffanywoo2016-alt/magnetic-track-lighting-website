Contact Area & Locking Design

Contact area and locking design directly affect connection stability and lifespan. Higher contact area + reinforced locking keeps the module firmly on the track.

End Cap Fixing Method

Screw-fastened end caps provide a tighter fit and better sealing than glue-fixed caps, reducing looseness and dust ingress over time.

Quality Control

Real Production & Quality Checks

Before shipment, every order is checked against the confirmed specification — track platform, module appearance, electrical connection, driver configuration and packing details.

◉ Track & Module Compatibility Check
Modules are fitted to the specified track and powered on before packing.
◉ Module Assembly & Component Check
Appearance, contact parts and assembly condition are checked by batch.
◉ Laser Marking / Product Identification
Custom logo, labels and identification marks are verified before assembly.
◉ Aging Test Before Shipment
Modules are powered on and run for a set period to confirm stable operation before packing.
