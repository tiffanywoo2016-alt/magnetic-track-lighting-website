Flood Light

Uniform linear flood lighting for general illumination in residential, retail and commercial projects. Available across all Tracklinear track systems.

Flood Grid Folding Grid Spot Pendant
Application

General Illumination with Wide Coverage

Flood light modules deliver a broad 120° beam angle, making them ideal for ambient and general lighting applications. Unlike focused spot lights, flood lights evenly illuminate large areas without harsh shadows or glare spots.

Common applications include living rooms, open-plan offices, retail showrooms, hotel lobbies and corridor lighting where uniform horizontal illumination is the primary requirement.

Key Features

  • ◉ 120° wide beam angle
  • ◉ CRI ≥80 – ≥90
  • ◉ Power range: 12W – 45W
  • ◉ Length: 300mm / 600mm / 1200mm
  • ◉ Compatible with 20mm / 23mm / 35mm tracks
  • ◉ Suitable for residential and commercial use
