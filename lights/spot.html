Application

Adjustable Accent Lighting

Track spot lights deliver focused, adjustable illumination ideal for accent lighting applications. With beam angles of 24° (narrow) and 36° (medium), they provide precise control over light placement, making them perfect for highlighting merchandise, artwork, architectural details and featured displays.

Common applications include retail stores, art galleries, museums, showrooms, hotel lobbies and luxury villas where visual emphasis and drama are key design objectives.

Key Features

  • ◉ Beam angles: 24° / 36°
  • ◉ CRI ≥80 – ≥90
  • ◉ Power range: 10W – 24W
  • ◉ Compact round form factor
  • ◉ Compatible with 20mm and 23mm tracks
  • ◉ Ideal for accent and display lighting
