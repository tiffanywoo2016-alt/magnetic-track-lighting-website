Pendant Light

Decorative suspended lighting for counters, tables, lounges and open spaces. Available exclusively for 23mm track system.

Flood Grid Folding Grid Spot Pendant
Application

Focused Decorative Lighting

Pendant light modules deliver a focused 24° beam angle in a stylish suspended form factor, making them ideal for accent and task lighting. The hanging design adds visual interest while providing precise illumination for specific zones.

Common applications include dining tables, kitchen islands, retail counters, lounge areas, reception desks and bar tops where a combination of decorative appeal and targeted lighting is desired.

Key Features

  • ◉ 24° focused beam angle
  • ◉ CRI ≥90
  • ◉ 8W power consumption
  • ◉ Dimensions: Φ41×267mm
  • ◉ Compatible with 23mm track only
  • ◉ Tunable white: 3000-6000K
