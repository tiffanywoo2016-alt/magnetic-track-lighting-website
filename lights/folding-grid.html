Folding Grid Light

Flexible angle adjustment with low-glare design for display, retail and hospitality lighting. Available in 20mm, 23mm and 35mm track variants from cost-effective to high-end commercial series.

Folding Grid

Adjustable Anti-Glare Lighting

Folding grid lights combine the low-glare performance of a grid optic with a flexible folding mechanism that allows precise angle adjustment. The compact form factor makes them ideal for accent lighting in retail displays, museum exhibits, hospitality settings and high-end residential interiors where glare control and directional flexibility are both required.

Available across three track platforms with multiple performance tiers to match your project budget and quality requirements.
