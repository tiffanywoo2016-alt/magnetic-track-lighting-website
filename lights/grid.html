Anti-Glare Linear Lighting

Grid Light

Grid lights are low-glare linear lighting modules designed for magnetic track systems. Featuring anti-glare optics and a precise 24° beam angle, they deliver comfortable, focused illumination ideal for office workstations, hotel corridors, retail displays and premium commercial environments.

Available for 20mm, 23mm and 35mm track systems with power ratings from 10W to 45W.
