Tracklinear / Track Lights

Magnetic Track Light Modules

Five light types — flood, grille, folding grille, spot, and pendant — compatible across 20mm, 23mm, and 35mm track systems. High CRI ≥90, tunable CCT, multiple beam angle options.

Flood

Flood Light

Wide-beam magnetic track light module for general area illumination. Available in 22Y, 20B, 30Y, 30B series.

▸ 120° beam angle ▸ 12-24W ▸ CRI ≥90
Grille

Grille Light

Anti-glare grille design ideal for office and retail applications. Compatible with 22Y, 20B, 30Y, 30B series.

▸ 30° / 60° beam ▸ 12-24W ▸ Anti-glare
Folding Grille

Folding Grille Light

Adjustable-angle folding grille design for directional accent lighting. Available in 22Y and 20B series.

▸ Adjustable angle ▸ 12-24W ▸ CRI ≥90
Spot

Spot Light

Narrow-beam accent lighting for highlighting displays, art, and architectural features. Compatible with 30Y series.

▸ 24° beam angle ▸ 18-24W ▸ CRI ≥90
Pendant

Pendant Light

Suspended magnetic track light module for decorative and task lighting over counters, tables, and reception areas.

▸ Suspended design ▸ 12-24W ▸ CRI ≥90
